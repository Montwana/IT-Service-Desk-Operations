# Ticket Prioritisation

## Overview

Ticket prioritisation helps the IT support team determine which incidents require attention first. At Botabota Office Solutions, tickets are assessed based on their business impact and urgency.

## Priority Levels

### High Priority

Issues that significantly disrupt business operations or affect multiple employees.

**Examples:**
- Company-wide network outage.
- Multiple employees unable to access essential business systems.
- Critical business application unavailable.

**Response:** Investigate immediately and prioritise service restoration.

### Medium Priority

Issues that affect an individual employee or a specific department but do not significantly disrupt the entire organisation.

**Examples:**
- An employee unable to access Outlook.
- A departmental printer not functioning.
- A user experiencing recurring application errors.

**Response:** Investigate promptly and resolve within an appropriate timeframe.

### Low Priority

Issues that have minimal impact on business operations and do not prevent employees from completing essential tasks.

**Examples:**
- Software installation requests.
- Minor application configuration changes.
- General IT assistance and non-urgent enquiries.

**Response:** Schedule the request according to workload and available resources.

## Impact and Urgency Assessment

| Assessment | Consideration |
|---|---|
| Impact | How many users or business operations are affected? |
| Urgency | How quickly does the issue need to be addressed? |
| Business effect | Does the issue prevent essential work from being completed? |

## Example: Incident INC001

**Issue:** Sarah Mokoena cannot connect to Outlook desktop.

- Impact: One employee affected.
- Urgency: Medium.
- Business effect: Email access through the desktop application is interrupted, but Outlook on the web remains available.
- Assigned priority: Medium.

## Important Considerations

- Priorities should reflect the actual impact and urgency of an issue.
- A ticket's priority may change if the situation worsens.
- Critical business disruptions should receive immediate attention.
- All priority decisions should be documented where necessary.
