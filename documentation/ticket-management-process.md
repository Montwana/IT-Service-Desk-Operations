# Ticket Management Process

## Overview

This document outlines the ticket management workflow followed at Botabota Office Solutions using Spiceworks Cloud Help Desk.

The process ensures that reported IT issues are recorded, investigated, resolved and documented consistently.

## Ticket Management Workflow

### 1. Ticket Creation

- Record the user's reported technical issue.
- Identify the requester and their department.
- Provide a clear and descriptive ticket title.
- Select the appropriate ticket category.
- Assign the ticket to the responsible technician.

### 2. Ticket Assessment

- Review the reported problem.
- Determine how many users or business operations are affected.
- Assess the urgency and potential impact.
- Assign an appropriate priority level.

### 3. Investigation and Troubleshooting

- Gather additional information from the requester.
- Review the symptoms and error messages.
- Perform relevant troubleshooting checks.
- Record investigation findings using internal notes.
- Keep the requester informed of progress.

### 4. Resolution

- Identify the cause of the issue.
- Apply an appropriate corrective action.
- Document the steps taken to resolve the problem.

### 5. Verification

- Confirm that the reported issue has been resolved.
- Verify that the affected service or application is functioning as expected.
- Record the outcome of the verification process.

### 6. Ticket Closure

- Communicate the resolution to the requester.
- Record the final resolution details.
- Update the ticket status to Closed once the issue is resolved and confirmed.

## Ticket Documentation Standards

Every ticket should contain:

- A clear problem description.
- Requester and department information.
- Category and priority.
- Troubleshooting steps performed.
- Internal investigation notes.
- Resolution and verification details.
- Final ticket status.

## Communication Guidelines

- Use clear and professional language.
- Acknowledge reported issues.
- Request relevant information when necessary.
- Provide progress updates.
- Document important interactions in the ticket.

## Simulation Disclaimer

This workflow is demonstrated through simulated IT support incidents using Spiceworks Cloud Help Desk. All company employees, reported issues and user interactions are fictional.
